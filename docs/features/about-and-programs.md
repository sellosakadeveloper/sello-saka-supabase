# About And Programs

## Purpose

The about and programs areas explain the organization and its offerings. They are closely related content pages and follow the same route-driven composition model.

## Relevant Source Areas

- `src/pages/About.tsx`
- `src/pages/Programs.tsx`
- shared header, footer, and reusable presentation components in `src/components/`

## Architectural Notes

These pages are public-facing and content-heavy. They should continue to rely on shared layout components and shared visual patterns rather than creating isolated page-only widget systems.
