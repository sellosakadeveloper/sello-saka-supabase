# Competition Compliance Copy Design

## Goal

Make the public compliance copy consistent across the site-wide footer, competition page, email ticket, and downloadable PDF ticket without overstating regulator endorsement.

## Approved Direction

- Use `NPC` instead of the stale `NPO Registration` footer wording.
- Keep `NLC: Scheme No: 00539/01` visible on competition-related surfaces.
- Link to the official National Lotteries Commission regulatory information page.
- Use factual wording that identifies the scheme and regulator without claiming blanket approval or endorsement.

## Wording Standard

### Footer

`(c) 2026 Sello Saka Foundation. All rights reserved. NPC.`

`NLC: Scheme No: 00539/01. Competition regulatory information is available from the National Lotteries Commission.`

### Competition Page

`Competition Compliance`

`NPC fundraising competition under registered scheme NLC: Scheme No: 00539/01. Regulatory information is available from the National Lotteries Commission.`

### Ticket, Email, and PDF

- Keep `NLC: Scheme No: 00539/01` in the ticket header.
- Add `NPC | NLC: Scheme No: 00539/01` in the stub/footer metadata.
- Add `Regulatory information: National Lotteries Commission` linked to the official regulatory page.

## Source Basis

- CIPC is the relevant official source for South African company registration terminology, including `NPC`.
- The National Lotteries Commission states that it regulates lottery competitions and that society lotteries must register and submit schemes.
- The wording therefore identifies the scheme and regulator, but avoids unsupported phrasing such as `approved by`, `endorsed by`, or `licensed by`.

## Scope

- Update public footer contact/compliance copy.
- Update competition page compliance block.
- Update shared ticket template used for email and PDF rendering.
- Update remaining stale public support email references where surfaced directly to users.

## Non-Goals

- No change to business logic, payment flow, or ticket data generation.
- No change to underlying scheme number handling.
- No legal interpretation beyond factual identification and official-link attribution.
